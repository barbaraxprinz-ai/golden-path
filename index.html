The Golden Path

Are you ON your path?
Switch yourself ON – like a light switch within you. Step by step: on and on and on.
Walk your golden path – the heart's journey.

A game of life. Walk the labyrinth together toward the Golden Heart – learn, grow and enjoy the journey. No one wins. Everyone arrives. You cannot win by speed – you can walk honestly, pause when needed, and arrive changed.

The goal: a pure golden heart.
A lamp to my feet, a light to my path – the labyrinth is lit step by step, not all at once. A path appears as you walk it.

Your path is not a finished story – it is a white canvas. You hold the brush. Every day is a new beginning.

Play, be still, ask, listen – and step forward.
Repeat.

✦ The spirit of the game

Golden heartlight – brightly, lightheartedly. Now and always, decide to invite and radiate light and love, and let both become the focus of all your thoughts, words and deeds. Once you step on this golden wisdom path, it changes your being, your relationships and your connections – starting with the one relationship to yourself.

Before enlightenment, cope with everyday life – and afterwards, too. There is always something to learn and to experience, and so you gain pearls of wisdom. Go with it, without resistance. Be courageous and take one step at a time – even baby steps take you forward. Life is like waves, with ups and downs. The labyrinth, an ancient symbol, keeps you on your path and shows the way to the centre of your heart: to unconditional love, pure trust and joy. Walk with your partner, your children, your friends and family, and with loved ones beyond the veil, spirit animals and angels. Free your Self, follow your inner voice and find your golden heart. Day by day, play – or start anew.
– Barbara

Remember how to be a spiritual being. With anticipation, dedicate yourself to this unique game of experience – a game for you, for understanding your life's path, with all its lessons and your infinite choices for your next steps.

Nothing is pre-ordained. Everything is changing constantly.

The Golden Path is meant to be helpful for humanity. It asks three questions: What do you love doing? What does the world need? What am I here to do?

It's not about winning. It's about gaining wisdom, insights and experiences – and enjoying our soul's expansion and expression. Crises and changes are chances: every feeling can be transformed, and transformation is always possible.

40 steps to love. Your life is a gift – you may choose, so choose wisely. Stay connected to higher consciousness and to your loved ones, and set the intention to focus on love. Some lessons are just for you: give your best, grow, explore, find peace within, spread your wings and free your Self.

This board is a map to go home: every step leads you back to the Golden Heart, where you came from.

The board is a field of possibilities – without fear, without limits. Stay here and now, walk each step consciously, and watch how many doors open.

How to play

Every step can become a Pearl of Wisdom. The question behind each move: What does this step show me about my own path?

Quick start

  1. Choose your archetype and begin together.
  2. Roll the dice and walk the golden path.
  3. Read the card aloud – answer, share, smile.
  4. Help each other on the way – that is how the light grows.
  5. Everyone arrives in the Golden Heart. No one wins – everyone grows.

The Golden Values

The Golden Path is life in steps; its goal is the Golden Heart. On the way you reach the 37 Golden Values – strengthening words ending in “-on”: the 12 gate qualities (Passion, Communication, Meditation, Regeneration, Compassion, Transformation, Intuition, Imagination, Inspiration, Innovation, Intention, Cooperation) and 7 values from the Golden Steps (Creation, Motivation, Connection, Devotion, Celebration, Illumination, Elevation), and 18 values of the path: Initiation (setting your intention), Observation (Here & Now), Expansion (Field of Possibilities), Manifestation (Blank Canvas), Revelation (Breath of Wind), Restoration (The Chalice), Invitation (Your Guides), Expression (Become the Teacher), Reflection (Walk It Again), Appreciation (Web of Love), Liberation (Alchemy of Feelings), Dedication (Walk Your Path), Dissolution (an obstacle dissolves), Congregation (Togetherness – walking side by side with like-hearted people), Transition (stepping out among the stars), Satisfaction (the traffic light), Evolution (growing to a new stage) and Emanation (arriving in the Golden Heart). At the end you see which values the whole circle reached together.

This game was never meant to be won. It was meant to be lived. The treasure was always within you.

The True Path

Deep down in our heart and soul we know the true way of living: connected, supporting and helping others, guiding and serving, becoming the best person possible – humble, kind and loving. Pure golden heart wisdom. A conscious way of living life.

Goal

Celebrate life and reach the Golden Heart in the centre – together. Every step, even a detour, is growth. You are never alone: your fellow players are your companions on the path, and help is always near. There are no winners. All pearls, gems, keys and tokens feed one shared Light. When the last traveller arrives, the journey is complete.

A turn

  1. Roll the dice and walk along the path (fields 1 → 40, then through the Golden Gate into the Heart).
  2. The field you land on tells you what happens. Read the card aloud, answer, share, decide.
  3. Receive your reward and pass the dice to the left.

The fields

    The 13 Gates

    Twelve gemstone gates stand along the path, each with a quality – Passion, Innovation, Inspiration, Intuition, Compassion… Land on one, answer its question and take the gemstone. Already have it? Take a pearl instead. The thirteenth gate is the Golden Gate before the Heart.

    Loops

    At the turning points of the labyrinth you may take a Loop of Reflection: you pause for one turn to rethink a decision, draw a Loop card and receive a pearl. Choice Cards also offer the loop – “I need time to feel this.”

    Golden Steps

    A golden step is a conscious time-out and a decision from the heart. It does not move you faster – it rewards you with a pearl and one of the seven Core Keys.

    Surprises on the path

    Flap of Wings cards move you: fly ahead, fly to the next gate, choose your lucky number, bring a companion to your field or give your steps away. And sometimes, at the end of a turn, a Sign from the Universe appears – a feather, a ladybug, a heart-shaped cloud, the number 11 – with a little gift for your journey.

    The dice

    Roll a classic dice 1–6 and move on. As a variation you can choose the wisdom dice: ✕ = take a break and breathe · −1 = step back one stone and deepen your wisdom · 1–4 = walk forward.

    Moments of Life & the inner voice

    Life has ups and downs: racing thoughts, fear, friendship struggles, stress, grief – and waves of joy. Moments of Life cards show how to transform them, and the support that appeared: an angel in the right moment, a sign, a word from a stranger, an advertisement, a car plate, a synchronicity. There is always support, even if we can't see it.

    Now and then you can pause with ♡ Listen to your heart: close your eyes, breathe, ask silently and hear the subtle inner voice. In stillness is power and strength.

    Held & Guided

    In between the steps, a short Word of Light may appear – an old, simple Bible word to remind us: we are guided, held, supported, embraced, never alone.

    Clear Communication

    On Clear Communication fields two travellers practise together: What is said? What is heard? What does the other person need in this moment? Is it clear and obvious? Repeat what you heard, listen with your heart, ask before you assume, try an I-message – and discover how much connection clear words create.

    Radiate Your Light

    How do we radiate our own light – the highest energy possible – in every step we take? By being content, steady, honest, loving, caring – and by reflecting. On Radiate Your Light fields you practise one of these qualities; your light lifts everyone at the table. On the board, each traveller glows: the fuller your energy battery and the more light you radiate, the brighter your aura.

    Number Wisdom

    Every number carries a meaning: 0 Pluto – transformation · 1 Sun – energy, healing power, empowering others · 2 Moon – emotions, mother, foresight, balance · 3 Mars – a powerful force that brings you forward (sometimes one step back is given) · 4 Mercury – bring your ideas into the world · 5 Faith – belief, mind, luck · 6 Venus – love, friendship, care, grace · 7 Saturn – cause and effect, step by step, grounding others · 8 Uranus – family, rebellion, sudden change · 9 Jupiter – communication, companionship, voice · 10 Medium – bridge between here and there. After every roll the meaning of your number lights up – and on Number Wisdom fields you learn more.

    Recharge Stations & your energy battery

    Every traveller has an energy battery ⚡. Sometimes we feel depleted or empty – then the little Recharge Stations help: breathe, walk, sing, dance, journal, create. Ask yourself: How do I charge my own battery in this moment? What do I need to re-charge or re-balance? Stillness and silence also fill the battery; hard moments of life use a little energy.

    Elevator · Lift Me Up

    Ten special Elevator cards stand for empowerment: someone believes in you, you remember your inner power – and you are lifted up, sometimes 5 or even 7 fields toward the light. Unique and special, just like you.

    Director's Chair

    You are the producer, director, author and actor in this film – the game of your lifelong journey. Whatever happens, you are co-creating together. Make the best out of every scene. Who do you want to be? What do you want to bring out into the world?

    Baby steps

    Choose “40 fields + 40 baby steps” for a gentler journey of 80 steps. Between the big fields lie little golden dots: a breath, a smile, a sip of water. Small steps count too – each one recharges your energy.

    Stepping Stones

    Sometimes the sunshine on the water hides the next stone. When you pause and look from another angle, new paths appear – left, ahead, right. It is encouraging to stop, pause and move forward in a new direction. Everything is possible.

    A lamp to my feet – the path lit step by step

    A little piece of our heart always feels the pull towards the centre of the game: love. The fine dotted lines from each traveller to the Golden Heart show this pull. Explore, expand, and ask yourself: what do you see? The labyrinth rests in soft twilight. Only the steps you have walked, and the next few steps in front of each traveller, are lit, like a lamp for your feet and a light for your path. You never see the whole way at once. And in the deepest darkness of the night you always see one small light: your soul light, the glow around your figure. You are always protected. Only from high above, for a moment, do you see how everything is connected.

    A path appears as you walk it. A thin golden thread grows behind you and connects the fields you have walked. Before the journey begins, each traveller receives three golden pearls: lessons meant only for them, marked on the board with a small pearl in their colour. Pick up your pearls in whatever order you need on your way back home. Everything else you choose step by step: will you follow the impulses that come to you, trust your guidance and your inner voice, or let yourself be swayed? The gates are gifts and special tasks.

    Rays of the Sun

    From the Golden Heart, rays of sunlight reach every traveller equally. The sun gives warmth, love and light to each and every being, without judgement or distinction. We may bend our knees before the sun in gratitude. Every journey has a field of the sun.

    Walk your path, the alchemy of feelings and the traffic light

    Walk Your Path cards give one short word for your next step: stay curious like a child, be courageous, breathe and step forward, stay open-hearted, you matter. Alchemy of Feelings lets you name the emotion that is here (anger, fear, sadness, envy, overwhelm and more) and find the gift inside it. When an obstacle comes: calm first, then ask what it is here to teach you. Sometimes a little traffic light appears after a roll. Green means connection, yellow means looking at the sunny side, and red means pause, breathe and listen within.

    The Web of Love and stepping out

    Love and light flow through a web of threads that connects everyone at the table. The more love you send (a heart hug, a blessing, forgiveness), the brighter the threads shine on the board. Love yourself first; love is a mirror. We are all different fingers of one hand.

    Time is only a rule of the board game, and our life here is a pit stop. Once per journey each traveller may ✧ step out at an exit point and choose: leave, learn and jump back in wherever you like; discover something among the stars and tell everyone about it; or stay in heaven as a winged messenger who sends light to the others (good when someone has to leave the game early).

    Magic from Within – you grow

    Whenever a traveller grows to a new stage (exploring, learning step by step, growing in courage, conscious and calm, walking in faith and trust), a Magic from Within card appears: a short piece of deep wisdom to take to heart. Put your hand on your chest. Your heart is the centre, the home and the goal. Love is always in each step you take.

    The City of Light and the twelve gates

    Imagine a city of light, shining like a precious jewel and clear as crystal. It has twelve gates: three in the East, three in the South, three in the West and three in the North. Each gate is made of a single pearl, an angel stands at every gate, and the gates are never shut. The street is pure gold, as clear as glass. The city needs no sun and no moon, because the Light itself shines there. Every tear is wiped away, and everything is made new. Each gemstone gate in the game has its own angel and a piece of deeper wisdom.

    Whoever arrives in the Golden Heart receives the Morning Star, the sign of a new day. Blessed are the pure in heart, for they will see the Divine. The wisdom here is meant for everyone, whatever their faith. Religio means to connect again.

    The core law of the game

    You cannot win by speed. You cannot walk another's path. But you can walk honestly, pause when needed – and arrive changed.

    The Initiation

    At the start of every journey you may read the Initiation aloud together: “Take a breath. Not to prepare – but to arrive. … You are not finished. You are unfolding. … Begin.”

    Your intention

    In the beginning, start your path consciously and with mindfulness. Each traveller sets an intention: what goal are you heading for, what do you want to experience? You are the master of your life – and there is also a plan that you may fulfil. In the Golden Heart your intention is shown again.

    Your guides

    You have supporters at your side – human beings here on earth and spiritual beings: angels, spirit animals and the dragonfly (drag on and fly – keep moving, be light). They give you strength and wonderful abilities to cope with all difficulties and lessons. You don’t have to see them – mostly you sense them, often in quiet moments: they calm, protect and support you. Dare to call them in. Whenever you are unsure, tap ♡ Heart and listen: your heart and your guides whisper what you need next.

    From high above

    Halfway along the path, each traveller looks down on the labyrinth as if from above, like a higher power, and sees how everything is connected. Every field you have walked lights up as a little dot of light. You may begin anxious, stumbling and unsure, then grow step by step: more courageous, conscious, calm, and full of faith and trust. Your light glows brighter as you go.

    Ruach · Breath of Wind

    In the Hebrew Bible the word Ruach appears almost 400 times. It can mean wind, breath or spirit, and also energy or life force. Air, wind, the North: higher consciousness, stillness, observing, clearing, cleansing. Respiration and inspiration: breathe consciously, relax, listen to the whispers and watch for signs.

    The Chalice

    Think of a pond fed by a living spring. Let wisdom flow from the Source and let it cleanse you. Invite new insights and new teachings, and do not hold on to old education and old information. Make yourself free and open to receive, and keep a balance between receiving and giving. Renew the wisdom within you again and again.

    Become the teacher, walk it again

    Life is full of signs: some point us towards good things, others towards lessons to learn. We may listen to teachers, learn our lessons and question the teachers, and so become teachers ourselves. Sometimes you walk the same path again and again until you understand the lesson. Sometimes you decide by yourself to stop, to go back, or to start again. Sometimes someone walks beside you for a while. You never walk alone.

    Here & Now

    The game invites you to stay in the moment: to walk each single step consciously and to observe. Here & Now cards bring you back to this breath, this sound, this step, these people.

    Field of Possibilities

    The game is a field of possibilities – without fear and without limits. These cards show one situation twice: once through the eyes of fear, once with an open heart. When we open up and recognise all the chances, life feels completely different from staying in fear or limitation. Choose possibility. Fear brings us to isolation. Love brings us together.

    The Blank Canvas

    This life path is not a story that is already written. It is a white canvas – we hold the brush. On Blank Canvas fields you choose a colour for your next step: Red for courage, Orange for creativity, Yellow for joy, Green for growth, Blue for calm, Violet for intuition, Gold for love. Each colour brings its own gift – and your palette grows with you. Every day is a new beginning.

    Every journey is new

    At the start of every journey the fields are shuffled anew: three treasure chests, portals, funny moments, a blank canvas and all the other fields appear in new places, the hidden chambers hide somewhere else, and the cards are drawn without repeating until a deck is used up. No two journeys are alike.

    Funny Moments & Happy Coincidences

    Life is also full of laughter: two tiny kittens behind a bush, a friend who calls just as you think of her, the bus that waits for you, the keys that were in your hand all along. Sometimes a surprise arrives: a beautiful letter, a loving e-mail, a cup of tea or a hot chocolate, flowers at the door, a funny notepad – little things that brighten the day. Funny moments appear on the path and as surprises – laugh together, make silly sounds, and enjoy the little gifts of life.

    Obstacles & Closed Doors

    As in real life, sometimes something stands in the way: a closed door, a fallen tree, thick fog, a wall of fear. They appear by chance after a roll. You always have choices: find the key within (answer the question and walk on), ask a companion for help, take another way, or wait and trust. Every obstacle is also a teacher.

    Hidden Chambers

    At the start of every journey, three hidden chambers are secretly placed in the labyrinth. Land on one and you discover it: a Chamber of Rest to recharge, a Chamber of Deep Learning, or a Chamber of Light. Once found, it stays visible on the board – and everyone may visit it.

    Portals & Journeys

    Sometimes a portal opens on the path. Step through and travel for a few steps to another place – a forest path, a crystal cave, a mountain summit, the ocean shore, the stars, an enchanted garden, the desert of silence, a hidden waterfall, a temple of light or a village of friends. Three magical steps, three questions – and you return with a gift.

    Treasure Chests

    Discover treasure chests on the path. Each one holds a little riddle from real life – a missed bus, a rainy day, a broken cup. Which choice brings more meaning? Find the little happiness – the ladybug on a stone, a helping hand, a rainbow after the rain – and the chest opens with a gift.

    Bridge of Light & Treasures

    Bridge of Light cards let you playfully feel the connection between here and there – loved ones, guides, signs, inner knowing. Treasures are discoveries on your path: a golden key, a pearl, a compass, a lantern. Every card asks a reflection question – and moves you on.

    Walking together

    Before you roll you may walk together with a companion (your roll moves you both), inspire someone or give them an answer, or choose silence and pause a round in the loop. Life is the game – the Golden Path is life in steps, full of chances to learn, explore, create and connect. There is no winning and no first place: the growth of the soul is what counts.

    Knowledge Tokens

    Whenever you help another traveller – gift a pearl, walk someone forward, send light from the Heart – you receive a Knowledge Token.
