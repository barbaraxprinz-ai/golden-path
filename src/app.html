Walk the Golden Path of life toward the Golden Heart – and reach the values of the soul: Intuition · Creation · Passion · Motivation · Inspiration · Compassion · Connection …

✦ Shuffle the dots ✦

Walk the seven circuits of the labyrinth toward the Golden Heart. Every step is an experience, every experience a Pearl of Wisdom. Pass through twelve gemstone gates and the Golden Gate, pause on golden steps, take loops to rethink a choice, and help each other along the way.

You never walk alone. Whatever step you take, you grow – and the others walk with you. Everything is connected: every pearl you gather lights the Golden Heart for everyone.

No one wins against anyone. The journey is complete when everyone has arrived – together.

How to play

Every step can become a Pearl of Wisdom. The question behind each move: What does this step show me about my own path?

The Golden Values

The Golden Path is life in steps; its goal is the Golden Heart. On the way you reach the 19 Golden Values – strengthening words ending in “-on”: the 12 gate qualities (Passion, Communication, Meditation, Regeneration, Compassion, Transformation, Intuition, Imagination, Inspiration, Innovation, Intention, Cooperation) and 7 values from the Golden Steps (Creation, Motivation, Connection, Devotion, Celebration, Illumination, Elevation). At the end you see which values the whole circle reached together.

Goal

Celebrate life and reach the Golden Heart in the centre – together. Every step, even a detour, is growth. You are never alone: your fellow players are your companions on the path, and help is always near. There are no winners. All pearls, gems, keys and tokens feed one shared Light. When the last traveller arrives, the journey is complete.

A turn

  1. Roll the die and walk along the path (fields 1 → 40, then through the Golden Gate into the Heart).
  2. The field you land on tells you what happens. Read the card aloud, answer, share, decide.
  3. Receive your reward and pass the die to the left.

The fields

    The 13 Gates

    Twelve gemstone gates stand along the path, each with a quality – Passion, Innovation, Inspiration, Intuition, Compassion… Land on one, answer its question and take the gemstone. Already have it? Take a pearl instead. The thirteenth gate is the Golden Gate before the Heart.

    Loops

    At the turning points of the labyrinth you may take a Loop of Reflection: you pause for one turn to rethink a decision, draw a Loop card and receive a pearl. Choice Cards also offer the loop – “I need time to feel this.”

    Golden Steps

    A golden step is a conscious time-out and a decision from the heart. It does not move you faster – it rewards you with a pearl and one of the seven Core Keys.

    Surprises on the path

    Flap of Wings cards move you: fly ahead, fly to the next gate, choose your lucky number, bring a companion to your field or give your steps away. And sometimes, at the end of a turn, a Sign from the Universe appears – a feather, a ladybug, a heart-shaped cloud, the number 11 – with a little gift for your journey.

    The die

    Roll a classic die 1–6 and move on. As a variation you can choose the wisdom die: ✕ = take a break and breathe · −1 = step back one stone and deepen your wisdom · 1–4 = walk forward.

    Bridge of Light & Treasures

    Bridge of Light cards let you playfully feel the connection between here and there – loved ones, guides, signs, inner knowing. Treasures are discoveries on your path: a golden key, a pearl, a compass, a lantern. Every card asks a reflection question – and moves you on.

    Walking together

    Before you roll you may walk together with a companion (your roll moves you both), inspire someone or give them an answer, or choose silence and pause a round in the loop. Life is the game – the Golden Path is life in steps, full of chances to learn, explore, create and connect. There is no winning and no first place: the growth of the soul is what counts.

    Knowledge Tokens

    Whenever you help another traveller – gift a pearl, walk someone forward, send light from the Heart – you receive a Knowledge Token.
